From Studio to Stage: What It Takes to Build a Sustainable Music Community

Wednesday, June 10, 2026 | 6:00pm - 8:30pm | Capital Factory, Austin, TX

Austin’s music scene doesn’t happen by accident.

​Behind every show, recording, festival, and creative experience are artists, producers, studio owners, promoters, educators, and community builders working tirelessly to create opportunities and keep local culture alive.

​This Prosper XO Town Hall explores the people, spaces, and communities that make Austin’s independent music ecosystem possible; and what it takes to sustain it for future generations.

​As cities evolve, venues close, costs rise, and technology continues to reshape how music is created and shared, how do we preserve the creative communities that make Austin special? What role do artists, studios, festivals, promoters, and supporters play in building a thriving cultural future?

​Join us for an evening of honest conversation, community connection, and insight from leaders actively shaping Austin’s music landscape.

Featuring:

Dustin Bozarth & Evan Bozarth
Co-Founders of Purple Bee Studio | Artists

Dustin and Evan are artists, producers, and co-founders of Purple Bee Studio, a creative space dedicated to supporting artists through recording, collaboration, community, and artistic development. Their work reflects a deep commitment to nurturing Austin’s independent music scene from the ground up.



Derek Moorhead
Music Producer | Artist | Promoter

Derek has spent years contributing to Austin’s music ecosystem as a producer, performer, and promoter. His unique perspective spans multiple sides of the industry, offering insight into artist development, live music, community building, and the realities of sustaining a creative career.



Kelsea Pinkston
Founder of BABE ATX | DJ Hipstir | Educator

Kelsea is the founder of BABE ATX, a community-driven organization focused on empowering women and underrepresented creatives through education, collaboration, and artistic opportunity. As DJ Hipstir, educator, and organizer, she has become a powerful advocate for inclusive creative communities and artist support.
