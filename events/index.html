Rock, Culture & Community: The Creative Journey of Luis Zapata

September 9th 2026 | 6:00pm - 8:30pm | TBA

What can music teach us about culture, identity, and the communities we build together?

Join Prosper XO for an evening with Luis Zapata; founder of Rock Lectures, Executive Director of the Pecan Street Festival, metal guitarist, and cultural historian whose career has been dedicated to exploring the powerful connections between music, history, and community.

From performing heavy metal to leading one of Austin's longest-running arts festivals, Luis has spent decades building creative spaces while uncovering the often-overlooked stories that have shaped rock and metal through his work with Rock Lectures.

This Town Hall will explore Luis's journey as a musician, educator, festival director, and community leader. Together, we'll discuss the role music plays in preserving culture, why festivals and creative spaces matter, what it takes to build strong artistic communities, and how the stories we tell shape the future of our cities.

Whether you're an artist, musician, educator, entrepreneur, student, or simply passionate about music and culture, this conversation offers a unique opportunity to learn from one of Austin's most dedicated advocates for the creative community.

At Prosper XO, we believe artists are essential cultural partners. Through our monthly Town Hall series, we bring together artists, educators, entrepreneurs, and community leaders to explore the ideas shaping the future of creativity, culture, and artist prosperity.

Join us for an evening celebrating music, history, and the communities that keep culture alive.

Featuring:

Luis Zapata
Founder, Rock Lectures | Executive Director, Pecan Street Festival | Metal Guitarist | Cultural Historian




Hosted by Lauren Bruno


Join us for an evening of honest conversation, community connection, and insight from leaders actively shaping Austin’s music landscape.


This event is free and open to the public.
