Austin’s music scene doesn’t happen by accident.

​Behind every show, recording, festival, and creative experience are artists, producers, studio owners, promoters, educators, and community builders working tirelessly to create opportunities and keep local culture alive.

​This Prosper XO Town Hall explores the people, spaces, and communities that make Austin’s independent music ecosystem possible; and what it takes to sustain it for future generations.

​As cities evolve, venues close, costs rise, and technology continues to reshape how music is created and shared, how do we preserve the creative communities that make Austin special? What role do artists, studios, festivals, promoters, and supporters play in building a thriving cultural future?

​Join us for an evening of honest conversation, community connection, and insight from leaders actively shaping Austin’s music landscape.

June 15, 2026
Musicians Master Class - Sync Licensing, Music Placement & Revenue Streams for Independent Artists

Music sync licensing has become one of the fastest-growing revenue opportunities for independent artists, songwriters, and producers — but many musicians still don’t understand how the process works, who controls the rights, or how to position their music for placement opportunities.

In this ATXM Master Class, musicians will learn the fundamentals of synchronization (“sync”) licensing and how songs are placed in film, television, commercials, video games, YouTube content, trailers, and streaming platforms. The session will break down the business side of sync in clear, practical language designed for working musicians navigating today’s evolving music industry.

Your music could be earning money in film, TV, commercials, games, and streaming content — if you understand sync licensing.

Join ATXM’s 2026 Master Class to learn how music placement really works and how independent artists can build sustainable revenue streams through sync opportunities.

About TALA

TALA offers free legal and accounting services to artists, craftspeople, and arts-centered nonprofit organizations from all creative disciplines, including visual arts, music, film and television, new media, writing and journalism, fashion design, theater, dance, and performing arts.



SIMS Foundation

The SIMS Foundation provides mental health and substance use recovery services and supports for musicians, music industry professionals, and their dependent family members. Through education, community partnerships, and accessible managed care, SIMS seeks to destigmatize and reduce mental health and substance use issues, while supporting and enhancing the wellbeing of the music community at large.
