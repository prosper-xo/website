Capital Factory in Austin, TX • ACL Room, 16th floor

The music industry often celebrates breakthroughs, viral moments, and success stories; but what does it actually take to build a sustainable career over the long term?

Join Prosper XO for an evening exploring artist development, community, career sustainability, building a business, and the support systems that help artists grow, evolve, and thrive.

From artist management and record labels to nonprofit support and community infrastructure, we'll discuss the realities of building a lasting career in music, the challenges artists face along the way, and what it takes to create an ecosystem where artists can succeed not just for a moment; but for a lifetime.

Whether you're an artist, manager, entrepreneur, student, industry professional, or passionate supporter of the arts, this conversation is for you.

Power to the Creator: A Cultural Campaign

Prosper XO launched Power to the Creator during SXSW 2026 on the streets of Austin Texas — a public awareness and cultural action campaign designed to shift how society values artists.

For too long, artists have poured everything into their work… only to release it into systems that extract from them rather than support them. This campaign brings that truth into the real world through posters and public activations — to spark conversation, awareness, and change.

About TALA

TALA offers free legal and accounting services to artists, craftspeople, and arts-centered nonprofit organizations from all creative disciplines, including visual arts, music, film and television, new media, writing and journalism, fashion design, theater, dance, and performing arts.



SIMS Foundation

The SIMS Foundation provides mental health and substance use recovery services and supports for musicians, music industry professionals, and their dependent family members. Through education, community partnerships, and accessible managed care, SIMS seeks to destigmatize and reduce mental health and substance use issues, while supporting and enhancing the wellbeing of the music community at large.
