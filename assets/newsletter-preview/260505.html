May 6, 2026

Capital Factory in Austin, TX • ACL Room, 16th floor

Artists give everything to their work, emotionally, mentally, and physically. But the systems surrounding music often fail to support the human behind the art.

This Prosper XO Town Hall, in honor of Mental Health Awareness Month, creates space for an open and honest conversation about what it truly takes to support artists as people; not just performers.
This is a safe, honest, and solutions-oriented conversation about how we better care for the people who create our culture.

​Featuring:

Derrick Lesnau — CEO, SIMS Foundation



Kay Cote — CEO, Amplify Austin



​Hosted by: Lauren Bruno & Prosper XO

Together, we will explore:


• The mental health realities artists face behind the scenes
• Burnout, pressure, and the emotional cost of creative work
• What support systems currently exist,and what’s missing
• The role of community in sustaining artists
• How we move from awareness to real, structural change

​This event is free and open to the public. Because when artists prosper, culture thrives.

SIMS Foundation

The SIMS Foundation provides mental health and substance use recovery services and supports for musicians, music industry professionals, and their dependent family members. Through education, community partnerships, and accessible managed care, SIMS seeks to destigmatize and reduce mental health and substance use issues, while supporting and enhancing the wellbeing of the music community at large.

Practical Tips for Effective Nonprofit Management, the third part of our Nonprofit Essentials Learning Series.
Wednesday, May 6, 2026 from 6:00 - 7:30 PM Central



Effective management is key to running a successful and compliant nonprofit organization. Whether you’re exploring nonprofit leadership, stepping into a new role, or have years of experience, this session will provide practical information and resources to help you operate efficiently so you can focus on advancing your mission.

We’ll cover essential topics, including:

  • Intellectual property considerations for nonprofits
  • Maintaining mission alignment and avoiding scope creep

  • Overview of required tax and business filings
  • Hiring best practices, including contractor vs. employee classification
  • Social media policies and risk management
    • And more!

Join us for a comprehensive overview of the key operational and legal considerations every nonprofit leader should understand.
